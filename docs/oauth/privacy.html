Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

MediaOverload is a personal, locally run application. This policy explains how it handles information when its owner connects a YouTube account.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, MediaOverload accesses the YouTube account permissions needed to upload videos through the YouTube Data API. It may send a selected video, title, description, tags, and privacy setting to YouTube when the owner starts a publish action.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer for future authorized uploads. They are not intentionally uploaded to this website or committed to the public GitHub repository. Local credentials remain until the owner deletes them or revokes access.

Sharing and use

MediaOverload does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties. Data is used only to perform the owner-requested media workflow and communicate with Google and YouTube APIs.

Your controls

The owner can revoke MediaOverload's access from the Google Account third-party connections page and can delete the local credential files. After revocation, the application cannot publish until authorization is granted again.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application behavior changes. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the project issue tracker.
